Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-14

1. Overview

Device Information values your privacy. The App does not require account registration or sign-in.

2. What information may be accessed

To display device diagnostics, the App may read the following data locally on your device:

  • Device and system details: brand, model, Android version, CPU, display specs, sensors;
  • Network details: connectivity status, Wi-Fi SSID, BSSID, IP, DNS, and link properties;
  • Battery and thermal details: level, voltage, current, temperature, and health status;
  • Storage and memory details: storage capacity, memory usage, and system stats;
  • Installed app details: app name, package name, version, signature digests, declared permissions, and components.

3. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions for the corresponding features:

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE for network and Wi-Fi status;
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION to read current Wi-Fi SSID on Android 10+;
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES to enumerate installed apps and show app details;
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION (Android 10+) to read step counter sensors and display them in the App.

4. Data transmission

All above data is processed locally on your device only and is never uploaded or transmitted to any server. Because we operate no companion servers for this processing, no copy of diagnostic readouts is retained on our systems.

5. Data retention

How long information is kept depends on whether it stays only in memory versus being saved by the App:

  • Device diagnostics (items in section 2): These values are read and shown on demand while you use the App. They are not uploaded and are not kept as a persistent history database by us. They may exist only transiently while a screen is open (similar to ordinary app memory).
  • Small local preferences on your device: The App may store a few settings locally (for example, whether you have accepted this policy and optional ruler or microphone calibration values). These entries remain on your device until you remove them using the deletion options below or until you uninstall the App. We do not set a separate “retention period” on servers for them because they never leave your device.

6. Deleting data

You can delete or reset data as follows:

  • Inside the App (saved calibrations): Open Ruler calibration or the ruler screen and choose the reset/control that restores the ruler to system defaults (for example “Reset to system default”). Open Microphone calibration and use “Reset to default” to remove a saved SPL offset.
  • Remove all locally stored App data: In Android settings, open Apps → Device Information → Storage (wording varies by manufacturer) → Clear data / Clear storage. This wipes local preferences—including consent and calibration—without affecting other apps.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device.

Feedback you voluntarily submit via Google Forms is handled under Google’s policy, not retained by our App beyond what Google’s product shows you after submission.

7. Third-party pages

The "Feedback" entry opens Google Forms. Any information you submit there is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy.

8. Policy updates

If app features change and affect privacy practices, this policy will be updated inside the App.
